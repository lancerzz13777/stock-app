What the agents learned from their losses

Every evening each agent checks its trades of the last 90 days for a condition it keeps losing in (time of day, market direction, the stock's move, volume). A pattern counts only if it lost in both the older and the newer half of its trades; then the agent skips that condition. Its trade size follows its record (25%–150%).
Paper trading only: no real money, no real orders. One day proves nothing; judge an agent over weeks. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
